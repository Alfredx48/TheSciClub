title: Every invite in one list
surface: Admin
type: new

The admin site's Invites lists every invite email and every QR code and invite link, newest first: who sent or made it, whether an email was opened and used, the member it became, and everyone who joined from each code.
Before, emailed invites were grouped by sender and day in the app's Admin, and codes were listed only to the person who made them.
