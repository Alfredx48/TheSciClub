- Peers: members browse peer profiles with filters, a profile survey, organization logos on cards and mentor marks.
- Joining: new people are invited, see a welcome screen and answer a short onboarding; the club is adults only.
- Events: a calendar filled on a schedule from other organizations' listings, where members mark Interested or Going.
- Me: a rebuilt Me page with a way to sign out, text size settings, and a screen where mentors manage their invites.
- Admin: administrators manage the invite list, make people mentors, and vouch for, pause or remove members.
- Behind the scenes: a starting directory of SCI organizations with photos hosted by the club, and access limited to members.
