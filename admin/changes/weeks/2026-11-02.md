- Events: organizers can make private events (invite only, or for chosen organizations), add co-hosts, and make a one-off event repeat.
- Events: members can share an event into a chat or elsewhere, and online events show Join online.
- Chat: conversations are split into direct chats and groups, headed by day, and long ones load the newest 100 messages first.
- Peers: members can link as family (both must agree), and about 190 more organizations were added with descriptions and logos.
- Notifications: a busy conversation pushes at most every two minutes, not to someone who has it open, and group pushes name the group.
- Joining and iPhone app: sign-in leads with Google or Apple, and the iPhone app opens every club link.
- Behind the scenes: administrators see how members arrived and page speed charts, and Chat, badges and the directory load faster.
