- Joining: invites open on a Welcome page saying who sent them, and joining takes fewer steps.
- Chat: a Marketplace (For sale, Giving away, Looking for) shows listings as a grid of pictures with the poster's location.
- Chat: members can react with any emoji and see who reacted; addresses, emails and phone numbers become links; unsent drafts are kept.
- Notifications: quieter by default, each conversation can be All, Only mentions or Off, and group chats have Important messages.
- Events: organizers get a live guest list with check-in that stays open a day, and can invite people outside the club from a list.
- Peers: an Include filter covers staff, family and friends, mentors and members unsure of their level.
- iPhone app: suggested only after signing in, with one-tap sign-in handed over from the browser.
