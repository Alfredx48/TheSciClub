- Home: the app opens on a Home feed of recent topics, events, photographs and people, which members can filter, like and post from.
- Chat: members can like and reply to posts, edit their own, rename groups, and write in any open room without joining.
- Me: members can choose Light, Dark or Match my phone, and can delete their own account, with a list of what goes and what stays.
- Joining: a Privacy Policy and Terms are open to everyone, and onboarding moves on faster.
- Peers: a Your answers page shows every survey answer, photos can be described for screen readers, and all photographs are private to members.
- Notifications: members are told when someone replies to or likes their post, with a switch for each.
- Behind the scenes: limits on what members can type, tighter database protections, and the move to thesciclub.com.
