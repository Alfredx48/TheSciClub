- Peers and Events: a List | Map switch shows events, member counts by city and organization logos on one map, with filters.
- Events: organizations can collect RSVPs, events get short links with QR codes, and reviews and follow-ups after events were added.
- Chat: posts can be edited until someone replies, @ mentions suggest and notify members, and posts can be shared outside the club.
- Joining: invite links and QR codes go straight in and expire after a day, people can ask for an invite, and passkey sign-in was added.
- Club partners: a trusted partner role can introduce two members and has its own page, numbers and printable QR card.
- Notifications: reminders go ten minutes and a day before events, and reactions notify authors.
- Admin and behind the scenes: org admins get a Stats & controls page, and the club serves its own map tiles.
